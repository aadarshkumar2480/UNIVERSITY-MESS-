About the project

The problem

At RBU hostels, menus sit on a notice board and feedback goes on paper that rarely reaches the mess committee.

The idea

One page for the menu and a short form for feedback, built to work on a phone in the mess queue.

Built with

HTML5, Bootstrap 5.3 grid, forms, cards and navbar, plus a little JavaScript. Designed first in Figma.
